Privacy Policy

This policy covers Bastet Agent Sync, including the Mac App Store edition named Bastet AI Sync by Bastet AI. It explains what the app handles when you choose to use Google Drive synchronization.

Data the app handles

You choose local agent sources and folders. The app may read selected conversation snapshots, settings, skills, agent and project information, and files needed for supported continuation. These sources can contain personal or sensitive information, including secrets within conversation text. The app also keeps local settings, permissions, sync journals, downloaded copies and prepared continuation profiles.

Google account details returned to the app, such as your display name, email address and permission ID, are used locally to identify the connected account and check that it matches your selected sync space. Bastet does not receive these details through an app-operated account service.

Google Drive and encryption

After you authorize Google and choose or create a Drive folder, sync begins only when you explicitly start it. The app requests the Google Drive drive.file scope, limited to files it creates or files you authorize for it. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Selected snapshot payloads and device reports are encrypted on your computer with XChaCha20-Poly1305 before upload. The recovery kit contains the key required to read them. Bastet does not have that key or a content server that receives these payloads. Anyone you give both the shared Drive access and the key can decrypt them.

Encryption does not hide all service data. Google can see your account connection, requests, folder names, generated file names and identifiers, types, sizes, counts and timing needed to operate Drive. Google processes that data under its own policies. This app uses Google API data only to provide the user-requested sync feature, follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and does not use Google user data for ads or general AI model training.

Local storage and recovery

OAuth refresh tokens and sync-space keys are stored in the operating system credential store; access tokens are held in memory while needed. On the Store edition, macOS folder permission bookmarks remain in local app configuration. Local source data, received replicas and prepared profiles can remain readable on the device; this is not whole-disk encryption.

Keep your recovery kit somewhere safe outside the shared Drive folder. Losing the space key and every recovery kit makes encrypted cloud content unrecoverable. Bastet cannot reset or recover the key for you.

Use, sharing and support

The app has no Bastet-operated content, advertising or analytics backend. Bastet does not sell your synced data, track you across apps or websites, or use your Google user data for general AI training. Data goes to the Google Drive space you select; copies may also be available to people you share that space and its key with.

If you voluntarily open a GitHub issue, the information you post is public and handled under GitHub's policies. Please do not post conversations, local files, tokens or recovery keys. Opening links from the app or this site takes you to the linked service, which has its own privacy practices.

Your controls and deletion

You can pause sync, deselect a source, change folder permissions, or forget the local Google login. Deselecting or pausing does not erase earlier copies. Forgetting the local login removes its locally saved token but does not revoke the Google grant; revoke it in your Google Account's connected-app settings if you want to end access.

Manage connected apps in your Google Account ↗

Local and cloud replicas remain until you remove them. To remove cloud copies, delete the relevant Bastet data from your Google Drive and review any shared copies or recipients. Remove local replicas, prepared profiles and source files through the app or your device's file controls as appropriate. Pausing, deselecting a source or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete Drive data or copies on other devices. Bastet cannot remove copies held by other people.

Product editions

The independently distributed desktop edition includes Agent Memory OS support and its own updater. The first Mac App Store edition is in preparation, excludes Agent Memory OS synchronization, and will receive updates through the App Store. The Store edition uses a separate local credential service. It has not been approved or published on the Mac App Store.

These Agent Sync pages

These Agent Sync pages use no analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts. Their host may retain ordinary access logs, such as IP address, request time, URL and browser details, to serve the pages and maintain security. The language selector stores your preference in this browser's local storage.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or support, use the public GitHub issue tracker. Share only information you are comfortable making public. We may update this policy as the product changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
