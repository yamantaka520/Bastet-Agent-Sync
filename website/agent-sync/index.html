A deliberate path between devices

Your choices stay in the loop.

01

Choose what moves

Select compatible local agents and data. Setup and Google authorization alone do not upload agent content; transfers begin when you start sync.

02

Use your Drive space

Bastet encrypts selected snapshots and device reports on your computer, then exchanges them through your chosen Google Drive folder.

03

Pick up carefully

Review available versions and supported continuation options. Conflicting branches are kept; an active agent session is not overwritten.

Clear boundaries

Built for local agent work.

Support depends on the agent, its local format, and the device. Bastet does not move a running process, guarantee every attachment, or restore cloud-hosted chats as native local sessions.

About the Mac App Store edition

Bastet AI Sync by Bastet AI, the first Store edition, is being prepared. It uses folder permissions chosen through macOS, receives updates through the App Store, and excludes Agent Memory OS sync. Signed sandbox acceptance and App Review remain pending.
